# Legends of Avantris: Complete Character & Cast Encyclopedia

This comprehensive encyclopedia details the player characters, notable NPCs, villains, and class options across all **Legends of Avantris** campaigns, including details from their folk horror D&D setting ***The Crooked Moon***.

---

## 1. Main Campaign Cast Directory

### Once Upon a Witchlight (Prismeer Feywild)
*   **Kremy Lecroux** (Played by Richie Gilder)
    *   **Race/Class:** Lizardfolk (Alligator subrace), Warlock (Death Loa Patron).
    *   **Lore:** The charismatic, flamboyant ringleader of the Carnivàle Lecroux. Kremy makes bargains with the dead and utilizes voodoo-inspired magic to swindle wealthy nobles.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"A contract signed in blood is just a friendly agreement between business partners, cher!"*
*   **Gideon Coal** (Played by Mace Caffrey)
    *   **Race/Class:** Fire Genasi, Fighter.
    *   **Lore:** The muscle of the carnival. Gideon has a fiery temper and heavy chains wrapped around his arms which he uses as weapons. He is famously responsible for killing Chuckles the Clown in a bar brawl.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"I don't care if you're a fae lord or a swamp hag—you step in my way, you're getting the chains."*
*   **Gricko Grimgrin** (Played by Mikey Gilder)
    *   **Race/Class:** Goblin, Druid (Circle of the Moon).
    *   **Lore:** An illiterate, hyperactive goblin who grew up feral in the woods. Gricko frequently wildshapes into various swamp critters and was once comically crowned the "Monarch of the Frogs."
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"Gricko eat the shiny bug? Gricko eat the shiny bug!"*
*   **Morning Frost** (Played by Derek Hudson)
    *   **Race/Class:** Tiger Tabaxi, Sorcerer (Mystic Mind Origin).
    *   **Lore:** A highly dramatic and colorful tiger caster who values aesthetics above all else. He is the magical spark of the Carnivàle Lecroux.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"Darling, your fashion sense is a worse curse than anything the Hourglass Coven could concoct."*
*   **Chuckles the Clown** (NPC/Spirit)
    *   **Lore:** A chaotic, disturbing jester who was killed by Gideon Coal. His spirit now haunts the party as a malevolent, laughing poltergeist that pops up at the most inconvenient times.

### Edge of Midnight (Druskenvald Folk Horror)
*   **Lethica Nightborne** (Played by Derek Hudson)
    *   **Race/Class:** Twilight Elf (formerly Dark Elf), Cleric (Stryga Loss Domain).
    *   **Lore:** A sombre, dedicated investigator who seeks to purge the curses plagueing the land of Druskenvald. She has personal ties to the dark mother Stryga.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"The mists do not hide the shadows; they are the shadows."*
*   **Jericho Sticks** (Played by Mikey Gilder)
    *   **Race/Class:** Scarecrow (formerly Warforged), Bard (College of Secrets).
    *   **Lore:** A wooden scarecrow brought to life by mysterious crop magic. Jericho gathers the secrets of the dead to power his songs and whispers them to unhinge his enemies.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"A secret is like a seed—bury it in the dirt, and eventually it will grow something wicked."*
*   **Briggsy Kratch** (Played by Richie Gilder)
    *   **Race/Class:** Jinxcursed (formerly Zombie), Warlock (Crossroads Patron).
    *   **Lore:** A walking corpse bound by a curse of bad luck. Briggsy made a deal at a literal planar crossroads to prolong his decay, trading fortune for survival.
    *   **Famous Quote:** *"I'm already dead, mate. The worst thing that can happen to me is I fall apart a bit faster."*
*   **Marius Renathyr** (Played by Andy Flynn)
    *   **Race/Class:** Dhampir, Blood Hunter.
    *   **Lore:** A lethal stalker of the night who balances his vampiric urges with his duty to protect the mortal villages of Druskenvald.
*   **Farryn of the Hartsblight** (Played by Kelsey Gilder)
    *   **Race/Class:** Satyr, Ranger.
    *   **Lore:** A corrupted woodland guide who tracks beasts that cross from the Feywild boundaries.
*   **Yorgrim** (Played by Mace Caffrey)
    *   **Race/Class:** Dwarf, Barbarian.
    *   **Lore:** A rugged miner who survived an encounter in the haunted deeps of the Skitterdeep Mine.

## 2. The Crooked Moon: Folk Horror Species & Subclasses

The sourcebook ***The Crooked Moon*** introduces custom species (Lineages) and subclasses representing the cursed building blocks of Druskenvald characters.

### Folk Horror Species (Lineages)
*   **Ashborn:** Humanoids touched by volcanic ash and embers, capable of exhaling smoke rings and resisting flames.
*   **Bogborn:** Peat-moss humanoids adapted to swamp environments, holding their breath indefinitely and slipping through mud.
*   **Graveborn:** Individuals partially raised from the grave, carrying a lingering deathly chill that deters beasts and undead.
*   **Threadborn / Relicborn:** Living dolls or construct-puppets stitched together from burlap, yarn, or clockwork. They do not need to eat or sleep and can stitch their wounds closed during rests.
*   **Harvestborn:** Humanoids bound to seasonal crops, featuring straw-like hair or pumpkin-carved features, capable of sensing rot and plants.
*   **Plagueborn:** Survivors of magical contagions who carry benign pathogens, making them immune to poisons and able to release toxic cough clouds.

### Custom Folk Horror Subclasses
*   **Occultist (Wizard):** Focuses on taboo glyphs, curses, and dark blood ritual magic.
*   **College of Whistles (Bard):** Bards who use eerie bone pipes and high-pitched whistles to summon fog and disorient minds.
*   **Sinner (Rogue):** A rogue subclass built around taking penalties to gain massive risk-based damage bonuses.
*   **Circle of the Old Ways (Druid):** Druids who draw power from ancient woodland totems, wicker effigies, and blood sacrifices rather than typical nature forces.
*   **Great Fool Patron (Warlock):** A chaotic warlock subclass representing a pact with a laughing, trickster fey entity (such as Chuckles' celestial prototype). Gives access to wild magic and chaotic laughing fits that stun targets.
